Welcome to Dominion! This is a guide to the basic features of the game.

--- The Goal ---
Dominion is a real-time strategy game about conquering territory and manipulating time itself. The ultimate objective is simple: be the last player with a General on the board. Capturing an opponent's General eliminates them and gives you all their land and armies.

Core Gameplay: Expand, Accumulate, Conquer

Your success depends on a simple loop:

    Expand: Capture new land to grow your empire.

    Accumulate: More land means more soldiers. Soldiers are your only resource.

    Conquer: Use your armies to defeat your opponents.

--- Movement and Combat ---

    To Move: Click and drag with your left mouse button from a tile you own to create a path. Your army will follow it.

    To Split Your Army: Right-click and drag to send only half the soldiers from a tile. The other half stays behind.

    Combat: When your army enters an enemy tile, the larger army wins.

    Snowballing: If your moving army crosses a friendly tile, it will pick up all the soldiers on that tile (leaving one behind) and add them to its ranks, creating a massive force.

--- The Map Tiles ---

    Land: Basic territory. Generates 1 soldier every 20 game steps when you own it.

    City: A valuable neutral tile. Generates 1 soldier every second game step.

    Forest: Hides the exact number of soldiers on a tile from your enemies.

    Mountain: Impassable terrain. Armies must go around it.

    General: Your most important unit. It acts like a City, generating 1 soldier every second game step. If you lose your General, you are defeated. You must lose all of your generals to be truly defeated.

--- Time Manipulation ---

The true power in Dominion comes from sacrificing armies from your General's tile to perform incredible time-based actions. These powers are accessed from the Timeline Controls panel on the left side of the screen.

The Timeline Tree

On the right side of the screen, you will see a visual tree representing all active timelines (or universes). You can only see and interact with one timeline at a time.

    Press 'Q' and 'E' to cycle between your active timelines.

    The currently selected timeline is highlighted in the tree.

Time Powers

    Split Timeline: Creates a perfect copy of your current timeline at that exact moment. You now have two universes to manage and attack from! Be warned: the more active timelines there are, the slower the game gets for everyone to give people time to manage their multiversal armies.

    Freeze Timeline: Temporarily pauses all activity in a timeline for a set duration. A great way to stop an enemy advance or buy yourself time.

    Overclock Timeline: Doubles the speed of a single timeline. Everything in it—army generation, movement—happens twice as fast.

    Open Portal (Hop): Creates a portal on a selected tile that links it to the same tile in another timeline. For a short time, your armies can travel between universes!

    Rollback Timeline: This is an "undo" button. It reverts a timeline to a point in its recent past, erasing everything that happened since.

    Anchor Timeline: Sets a "save point" in a timeline. Once an anchor is placed, no player can roll that timeline back to a point before the anchor.
